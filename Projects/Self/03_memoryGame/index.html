Rules

  • Game will start initially with 2 colours.
  • In level 1 you will see 4 nodes of that colour. You have 5 seconds to memorize it. After 5 seconds the nodes will be hidden and you have to replicate the pattern.
  • After each level the node increases by 1 (upto level 7)
  • From level 8 colour starts to increase but the nodes remain same.
  • You can play the game upto level 50.
